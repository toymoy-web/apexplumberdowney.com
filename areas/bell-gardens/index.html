Two Water Operators

The city and Golden State Water

The City of Bell Gardens operates about one third of the water system, in the northern part of the city, and serves roughly 2,112 customers. That supply combines City Well Number 1 with imported Metropolitan Water District water. Golden State Water operates the other two thirds, and the city contracts with Liberty Utilities for meter reading, billing, and distribution work in its own section.

Golden State Water's 2026 report, covering 2025 testing, lists hardness for the Bell-Bell Gardens system at 8.0 to 16 grains per gallon, with an average of 12. Its supply is a blend of treated Central Basin groundwater and imported water, and the imported part carries chloramine. We have not found a published hardness number for the city's own section.

Hard water at 12 grains per gallon leaves scale on heating elements and faucet aerators. For a household with a tank heater, a yearly flush helps, and our water softener installation and repair page explains sizing.

A rental tenant usually cannot install a softener, so the practical step is to tell the landlord when a heater fails early. Any heater we replace follows the California Plumbing Code, built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), including the relief valve and its drain line.

Before We Arrive

If you have a burst pipe or active leak

When water is loose in a Bell Gardens building, a few quick steps limit what spreads to the next unit. They take about two minutes and cost nothing.

1

Shut off the nearest valve

Under a sink or behind a toilet you will find a small oval or round shutoff. Turn it clockwise. If water is spreading from a wall, find the main shutoff or ask the manager where it is.

2

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near an outlet, a light fixture, or a water heater closet, switch off that breaker only when you can reach the panel dry. Never stand in water to do it.

3

Warn the neighbors

In a multi-unit building, knock on the door below and tell the property manager at once. Water moves down through ceilings quickly, and an early warning keeps the damage smaller.

4

Call our 24/7 line
