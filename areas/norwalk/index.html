Norwalk's Water

What is in the pipes before it reaches yours

Golden State Water Company's Norwalk System blends treated groundwater from the Central Groundwater Basin with imported water from the Colorado River and the State Water Project. Its 2026 report, covering 2025 testing, lists hardness of 8.0 to 30 grains per gallon with an average of 18. The range is wide, so two homes in the same city can see very different scale.

The practical effect is scale. In a tank water heater, scale settles on the bottom of the tank, wastes heat, and uses up the anode rod. On faucets and showerheads it shows up as white crust and weak flow. A yearly tank flush and an anode check are the cheapest protection, and our water heater troubleshooting team starts there when a Norwalk tank misbehaves.

The water Golden State Water buys from the Metropolitan Water District contains chloramine. The utility says dialysis patients, aquarium owners, and some businesses must remove it before use. It does not change how we repair your pipes, but it is worth knowing if you keep fish.

Renting in Norwalk

When the plumbing belongs to a landlord

About 47 percent of Norwalk's housing is renter-occupied, per census-based figures, which is a much higher share than most suburbs. That makes landlord and tenant plumbing calls part of everyday life here.

California's Civil Code section 1941.1 generally requires a rental to have plumbing in working order. If you rent and water is spreading, shut off the nearest valve, photograph the damage, tell the landlord in writing, and call us. If you manage rentals, we can work with you on repairs. Before We Arrive

If you have a burst pipe or active leak

A burst pipe or an active leak in Norwalk is a race against the floor. These four steps take about two minutes and can save drywall, carpet, and a landlord dispute.

1

Shut off the main valve

Most Norwalk homes have the main shutoff on the front wall near the hose bib or in the garage. Turn a wheel handle clockwise or a lever a quarter turn, and stop if it will not move.

2

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near outlets, a garage water heater, or a ceiling light, switch off that breaker only if you can reach the panel dry. Never stand in water to do it.

3

Clear the area

Lift rugs, boxes, and electronics off the floor and photograph the damage first. Renters should text the landlord a photo right away so there is a dated record.

4

Call our 24/7 line
