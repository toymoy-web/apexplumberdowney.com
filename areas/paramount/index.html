A City That Fought to Exist

From dairy land to dense blocks

Paramount incorporated on January 30, 1957, after a campaign called "Save Paramount for Paramount" fought off annexation by Long Beach, Bellflower, and South Gate. Dairies were still operating inside the city, and the last one did not close until 1977.

The practical result is the housing. Census-based data show that most Paramount homes were built in the 1950s, which means the plumbing in many of them is now about seven decades old unless it has been replaced.

A house that age deserves an inspection before any repair, because the cause of a symptom is often not where it appears. The California Plumbing Code, built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), expects an accessible shutoff valve at each fixture, and in houses that old those valves are often seized.

City-Run Water

How Paramount's water reaches your tap

The City of Paramount's Public Works Department supplies local water from deep wells and from imported Metropolitan Water District water. The city's 2018 report is the most recent official one we could confirm. It lists total hardness at an average of 190 milligrams per liter in the well water, about 11 grains per gallon, and about 10 in the imported supply. Both are in the hard range.

The same report says the well system filters manganese before the water is distributed. Manganese is a common cause of brown or black staining on fixtures, so that is a good sign, but scale is a separate matter. Hard water leaves white crust on faucets and builds up in water heaters, which is why a yearly tank flush matters. Our heater repair page explains the routine.

Because newer reports may differ from 2018, we treat these numbers as a guide. A test strip at your tap gives the number that matters for your house. Before We Arrive

If you have a burst pipe or active leak

In Paramount, an active leak often involves a neighbor as well as your own floor. These four steps keep the damage small and the paperwork clear.

1

Shut off the main valve

In a house, the main shutoff is usually on the front wall or in the garage. In a small apartment building, ask the manager which valve serves your unit, and close the shutoff under your own sink in the meantime.

2

Kill power to wet areas

If water is dripping near outlets or a ceiling light, switch off that circuit only when you can reach the panel dry. A wet ceiling below a unit upstairs can carry power risks, so stay clear of it.

3

Clear the area

Tell the neighbors below and the property manager right away, then lift belongings off wet floors. Photograph the damage so a renter or an owner has a record.

4

Call our 24/7 line
