Who Supplies Your Water

Two water systems inside one city

The City of Pico Rivera Water Division serves about 9,400 service connections and produces all of its supply from groundwater. The Pico Water District, formed in 1926, serves the rest of the city from six wells and a reservoir that holds 1.25 million gallons. The city's website has an address lookup map that shows which one serves a given street.

It matters because the utility owns the meter and the line up to it, and a pressure complaint, a high bill, or a water quality question goes to the right office only if you know which one it is. Our job starts on your side of the meter, and we can tell you quickly which side a problem is on.

The River Plain

How the city grew, and what it left behind

Pico Rivera sits on the flat plain between the Rio Hondo and the San Gabriel River. It was farmland until the end of World War II, when builders moved in, and it was incorporated on January 29, 1958 by merging the communities of Pico and Rivera. Pio Pico State Historic Park, where the last Mexican governor of California built his mansion, is part of the local landscape.

The useful lesson for a homeowner is the age of the housing. Most of it went up after the war, so the plumbing in a typical house is now seven or eight decades old unless someone has replaced it. That is a reason to inspect, not to panic. Our dispatch covers the whole city, from Smith Park on Rosemead Boulevard to Rivera Park on Shade Lane and Pico Park on Beverly Boulevard, and it also covers Downey next door.

Before We Arrive

If you have a burst pipe or active leak

A burst pipe or active leak in a Pico Rivera home is easier to contain than it feels. Work through these four steps in order, and call us while you do.

1

Shut off the main valve

The main shutoff on your side of the meter is yours, whichever utility serves you. The valve at the curb belongs to the utility, so leave it alone unless they tell you to use it.

2

Kill power to wet areas

Many Pico Rivera homes keep the water heater in the garage, so check for water near it before you touch any switch. Turn off the breaker only if you can reach the panel dry.

3

Clear the area

Move stored boxes away from the garage floor drain and lift rugs off wet floors. Photograph the damage first, since owners deal directly with their insurer.

4

Call our 24/7 line
