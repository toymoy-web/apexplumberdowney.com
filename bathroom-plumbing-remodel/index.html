Start With the Floor

What decides how much a bathroom remodel costs in plumbing

Bathroom plumbing and remodel work in Downey, CA means planning and installing the water supply, drains, valves, and fixtures for a new or updated bathroom. The single biggest factor in the plumbing scope is whether you are keeping fixtures where they are or moving them.

A like-for-like update, with the toilet, vanity, and shower in their old spots, uses the existing pipes. A new layout means new drains and new supply lines, and on a concrete slab that means cutting the floor. Knowing which you are doing early saves money.

Keeping the layout

  • Uses existing drains and supply
  • Lowest plumbing scope
  • Best time to replace aging valves
  • Shorter timeline

Changing the layout

  • New drains and supply routes
  • Slab homes need concrete cut
  • Venting and slope must be planned
  • Needs a permit and inspection

Rules to Know

What California expects of a new bathroom

California limits new fixtures to 1.28 gallons per flush for toilets and 1.8 gallons per minute for showerheads. The California Plumbing Code, built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), sets the rules for valve type, trap size, venting, and drain slope. Shower valves must be pressure-balancing or thermostatic, which is why a remodel is the right time to replace an old one.

Homes built before 1994 are also covered by California's SB 407, which requires water-conserving fixtures in older buildings and comes up at sale. This is general information, so confirm the details with your agent.

What a 1950s bathroom hides

Census-based counts put roughly 13,400 Downey homes in the 1950s. Bathrooms from that era often still have their original shutoff valves, shower valves, and tub drains behind the tile. Those parts are inexpensive to replace while the wall is open and costly to replace afterward.

Where water is hard, scale builds up quickly on faucets and showerheads. Choosing fixtures with removable aerators and easy-to-clean finishes helps. For a closer look at the tub and shower side of the work, see our shower and bathtub installation page, and for the toilet, toilet repair.
