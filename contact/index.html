Contact Downey Apex Plumbing

Call the 24/7 Downey line, or email for anything that is not urgent. There is no form to fill out.

  • Open 24/7

The fastest way to reach us is by phone. The Downey line is answered 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including holidays. There is no contact form on this site, because a call gets you an answer faster than a message you wait on.

If you prefer email for something that is not urgent, write to contact@apexplumberdowney.com. For anything involving water, gas, or sewage, please call instead.

What to have ready when you call

  • 1Your street address, or the nearest cross streets, and the city.
  • 2What is happening: leaking, backing up, no hot water, or something else.
  • 3Where it is happening: which room, fixture, or part of the yard.
  • 4What you have already done, such as closing a valve or turning off the heater.
  • 5Whether anyone is in danger or there is a smell of gas. If there is, leave and call 911 first.
  • 6A callback number in case the call drops.

If it cannot wait until morning

Call the 24/7 line and say what the water is doing: running, rising, or spreading. Say whether you have closed the main, and give the address and a callback number. Then close the main and keep power away from wet areas while we plan the visit.

For gas, call 911 or SoCalGas first. For a street-wide outage, call the City's Utilities Services Division. For everything else in the house, we answer the line.

How We Price

What to expect when you call

We ask enough questions to understand the problem, then tell you what we can quote by phone and what needs a look first. Estimates on non-emergency work are free. On an emergency call, you hear the price before the repair begins and you decide whether to go ahead.

For work such as sewer replacement, repiping, or gas lines, the price depends on a camera inspection or a site visit, so we do not guess by phone. We would rather tell you that than quote a number that changes. If water has already soaked floors or walls, our drying and restoration page explains the separate drying line.

We serve all of Downey, including ZIP codes 90240, 90241, and 90242, plus Norwalk, Paramount, South Gate, Pico Rivera, Santa Fe Springs, Bellflower, and Bell Gardens. If you are between those, call and we will confirm.
