What to do if you smell gas

  • 1Leave the house right away and take everyone with you.
  • 2Do not turn switches on or off, use a lighter, or start a car in the garage.
  • 3Call 911 and your gas utility from outside, or from a neighbor's home.
  • 4Do not re-enter until the utility says it is safe.
  • 5Call us afterward for repairs. We do not respond to a gas smell before the utility does.

Gas line installation and repair in Downey, CA covers new gas lines for ranges, dryers, water heaters, outdoor grills, and fire features, plus repairs to leaking or damaged piping on the house side of the meter. Every job is permitted, pressure-tested, and inspected before gas is turned on.

Gas work is different from water work, because a mistake can be dangerous. That is why we start with the permit and end with a test, and why we are careful about what we promise.

Why gas line work is mostly about age and load

Roughly 13,400 Downey homes date to the 1950s and about 3,000 more to the 1940s. Gas piping in those homes was sized for the appliances of that time. Adding a tankless water heater, a bigger range, or an outdoor kitchen can exceed what the original line can carry. A load check comes first.

The California Plumbing Code, built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), covers fuel gas piping, including materials, sizing, and testing. We follow it, and the city's inspection confirms it.

The gas utility in Downey, Southern California Gas Company, owns the meter and the line up to it. If the issue is on that side, you call the utility. If it is on the house side, that is where we come in.
