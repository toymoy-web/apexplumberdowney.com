Start With the Symptom

Hot water that comes and goes, or an error code on the display

Tankless water heater service usually starts with a hint, because these units rarely fail all at once. The hints are a shower that turns lukewarm, a short burst of cold, a flame that will not hold, or a code on the front panel. If your heater has been running for years without a flush, those hints usually point to the same cause, scale in the heat exchanger.

Another common situation is a heater that someone told you could not be serviced and should be replaced. That is sometimes true, but not as often as it sounds. Most problems can be diagnosed and fixed on the spot.

Tankless water heater service in Downey, CA is the maintenance and repair of on-demand water heaters, including descaling the heat exchanger, cleaning filters, checking venting and gas supply, and clearing error codes. It keeps the unit efficient and extends its life.

A basic maintenance schedule

  • 1Flush and descale the heat exchanger at least once a year in hard water.
  • 2Clean the inlet water filter screen at the same visit.
  • 3Inspect the vent for blockages, corrosion, and secure joints.
  • 4Check the condensate drain and neutralizer on condensing models.
  • 5Verify the temperature setting. Many units ship set high.
  • 6Keep the area around the unit clear of storage.
Local Water

Why hard water is hard on tankless units

The City of Downey Water Division draws on 20 groundwater wells in the Central Basin. Check its water-quality report for the current hardness figure. Hard water is the kind where scale builds on a tankless heat exchanger fast enough to matter within a year or two.

The scale acts as insulation, so the unit works harder for the same heat. It also narrows the passages, which cuts flow and triggers sensors. The longer scale sits, the harder it is to remove, so annual flushing is the least expensive protection.

For households that want to cut scale at the source, a water softener installation and repair helps. For the unit itself, annual service keeps it within spec.

Service or Replace

A fair way to decide

Usually serviceable

  • Unit under about ten years old
  • Codes tied to scale, filters, or venting
  • Heat exchanger is sound
  • No history of repeated failures

Worth discussing replacement

  • Heat exchanger is cracked or leaking
  • Board or sensors fail repeatedly
  • Unit is well past its expected life
  • Parts are no longer available
