Toilet repair and installation in Downey, CA covers running toilets, weak flushes, leaks at the base, cracked bowls, and new installs. Most repairs take under an hour, and the cost depends on which part has failed. If a toilet is overflowing, call our 24/7 line and close the valve behind the bowl first.

The tank holds more parts than most people expect. A flapper, a fill valve, a flush valve, a handle, and supply connections all wear out on their own schedule. The trick is knowing which one is failing, because replacing the wrong part wastes a visit.

Symptom Guide

What your toilet is telling you

SymptomMost likely causeUsual fix
Runs on or refills every few minutesWorn flapper or fill valveReplace the part and reset the water level
Weak or incomplete flushClogged rim jets, low water level, or a partial clogClear the jets, adjust the level, or clear the drain
Water around the base after flushingFailed wax ring or loose flangePull the toilet, replace the ring, reset
Tank sweats or drips onto the floorCondensation or a leaking tank boltReplace the bolts or add an insulating liner
Toilet rocks when you sitLoose closet bolts or a damaged flangeTighten, shim, or repair the flange
Constant hissing or phantom flushingFill valve or flapper leakReplace the valve or flapper

What to do if a toilet is overflowing

  • 1Lift the tank lid and push the flapper down to stop more water entering the bowl.
  • 2Close the supply valve behind the toilet by turning it clockwise.
  • 3Do not flush again. Do not use a chemical drain opener.
  • 4Put towels around the base and take photos if water reached the floor.
  • 5Call if the valve will not close or the water keeps rising.

Repair when

  • The porcelain is sound
  • A single part has failed
  • The toilet flushes well otherwise
  • The model is under about 20 years old

Replace when

  • The bowl or tank is cracked
  • It clogs repeatedly even with a clear drain
  • It uses more water than current rules allow
  • Parts are no longer available
