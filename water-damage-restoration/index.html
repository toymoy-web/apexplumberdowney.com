Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Downey, CA

Drying, cleaning, and repair after a pipe burst, heater failure, or backup, on a dedicated 24/7 restoration line.

  • Open 24/7
  • Dedicated restoration line

Water damage restoration in Downey, CA is the work of drying, cleaning, and repairing a building after water gets in, whether from a burst pipe, a failed water heater, a washing machine hose, or a sewage backup. Call the restoration line now if water is spreading, and the first steps will be extraction and drying.

Water damage restoration is a different job from plumbing repair, which is why this page has its own phone line. A plumber fixes what leaked. A restoration crew deals with what got wet.

What to do in the first hour

  • 1Stop the source if you can: close the main shutoff or the fixture valve.
  • 2Switch off power to wet areas only if you can reach the breaker without standing in water.
  • 3Photograph and video the damage before you move anything.
  • 4Move small items, rugs, and electronics to a dry place.
  • 5Open windows if the weather allows, and run fans if the power is safe.
  • 6Call your insurer, then call the restoration line.
How Bad Is It?

Water categories and why they matter

CategorySourceWhat it usually means
Category 1Clean source, such as a broken supply lineOften dried in place if caught quickly
Category 2Gray water, such as a washing machine or dishwasherNeeds cleaning, and porous materials may be removed
Category 3Grossly contaminated, such as sewage or floodwaterAffected porous materials are usually removed

The categories come from ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry standard for professional water damage restoration. The standard also classes a loss from 1 to 4 by how fast water will evaporate from the materials, which sets how much drying equipment is needed. The category and the class together decide the plan.

Emergency water damage restoration: the first 24 hours

The first day sets how far the damage spreads. Wet drywall, subfloor, and insulation begin to hold moisture within hours, and what starts as a stain can become mold if drying waits. Stop the source of the water first, then call the restoration line so drying can begin.

The California Department of Insurance claims guide tells homeowners to keep a log of calls, keep receipts, protect the property from further damage, and avoid extensive permanent repairs until the adjuster has seen the loss. It also advises getting written bids that list materials and prices line by line. Its consumer hotline is 1-800-927-4357. This is general information and not insurance advice.
