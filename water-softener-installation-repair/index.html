The Math

What Downey's hardness means for a softener

Choosing a water softener for a Downey home starts with arithmetic. The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency estimates average indoor water use at about 82 gallons per person per day. Multiply that by the number of people, then by the hardness in grains per gallon, and you get the grains the softener has to remove each day.

Third-party sources put Downey's hardness somewhere near 10 to 18 grains per gallon, and the city's own current report is the authority on the exact figure. The worked example uses 14 as an illustration, not a measured value. Your own number should come from a test strip or a lab test, since it can differ at your tap.

Salt-based softener

  • Removes calcium and magnesium by ion exchange
  • Stops scale at the source
  • Needs salt and a drain connection
  • May be restricted by some local agencies

Salt-free conditioner

  • Does not remove hardness minerals
  • Changes how scale forms
  • No salt or drain needed
  • Results vary, so set expectations
Before You Buy

Local rules and honest limits

Some California cities and sanitation districts limit salt-based self-regenerating softeners, because the brine they discharge raises the salt content of wastewater. That is a local rule, not a statewide ban. We tell you to check with the City of Downey and the sanitation district that serves your address before you commit.

When you shop, look for certification to NSF/ANSI 44, the standard for residential cation exchange water softeners, on the unit's label. A softener also does not filter water. It does not remove chlorine, contaminants, or the taste some people dislike.

For that you want a separate filter, which our water filtration installation page covers. If your goal is drinking water quality, a softener alone will not meet it.

Where a softener pays back

Census-based counts put roughly 13,400 Downey homes in the 1950s, and many have had a water heater replaced two or three times. Scale is one reason tanks wear out. A softener addresses the cause, while a flush and anode check address the symptoms.

A household that runs a dishwasher and washing machine daily will see the benefit in appliance life and cleaning time. For a tankless unit, a softener can cut how often it needs descaling. See tankless heater service for the details.
